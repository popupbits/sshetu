First release.

SSHetu is an SSH client: a real terminal, SFTP with a remote text editor, local and remote port forwarding, and keys you generate on the device. When the server has tmux, a dropped connection picks up where it left off.

No account and no backend. Hosts and keys stay on this device, credentials in the Android keystore. Import your existing ~/.ssh/config to get started.

The app is in English and Nepali.
